The ODP-Reuse dataset collection provides a curated suite of benchmark datasets for studying and evaluating the reuse of Ontology Design Patterns (ODPs) in ontologies. Each dataset in this collection corresponds to a distinct discovery source&mdash;(i) Paper &amp; Repository Search, (ii) GitHub Code Search, or (iii) DOS&ndash;DP template-based ontologies&mdash;and contains ontologies that have been verified to fully reuse at least one ODP under a strict 100% IRI-match criterion.&nbsp;The IRIs appearing in the ontologies and patterns are consistently hashed. This ensures that the datasets retain their exact structural and reuse relationships without exposing the original IRIs.
